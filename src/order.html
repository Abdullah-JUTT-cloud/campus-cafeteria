How to Order

Ordering at Campus Bites is simple. Just follow these four steps.

Steps to Place an Order

  1. Choose your items

    Check the menu or the menu board at the counter, and note down the names of the items you want.

  2. Pay at the counter

    Pay by cash, Easypaisa or JazzCash at the counter. Keep your receipt safe, you will need it.

  3. Collect your order token

    Take the token number given to you with your receipt. This is how we call your order.

  4. Pick up your order

    Watch the display screen for your token number, then collect your order from the pick-up counter.

Good to Know

Peak hours

Peak hours are 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Waiting time is longer at lunch, so come a little early or place your order before 12:00 PM.

Pick-up rules

  • Collect your order within 15 minutes of your token number being called.
  • Check your items at the counter before leaving.
  • Tokens not collected within 30 minutes are cancelled.
  • Eat-in trays must stay inside the cafeteria hall.
